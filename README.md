# The Impact of AI Adoption on Canadian Wages

A data analysis project exploring how artificial intelligence adoption has affected Canadian wages across provinces, gender, and education levels.

## Project Overview

This project investigates the contributors to AI's effects on Canadian wages through three subtopics:

**Subtopic 1 — Provinces:** Do wages in AI-related jobs grow differently across Canadian provinces? We found that urban provinces (Ontario, BC, Alberta, Quebec) experienced faster wage growth than rural ones, supporting the hypothesis that AI is widening the urban-rural wage gap.

**Subtopic 2 — Gender Equality:** Is there still a gender wage gap in tech-related occupations, and has AI made it worse? We found that women need to work 1–13% more hours than men to earn the same wage, and that the impact of AI on the gender gap remains inconclusive.

**Subtopic 3 — Education Levels:** How does education level influence representation in AI-related occupations? Workers with short-cycle tertiary education had the highest representation (~16%), while those with only a high school education saw a decline over time.
